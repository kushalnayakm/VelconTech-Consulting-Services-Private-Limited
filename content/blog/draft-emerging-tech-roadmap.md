> **Note:** This article is a draft for internal review. Content, dates, and recommendations may change before publication.

Technology roadmaps fail when they list every trend and commit to none. This draft outlines how enterprise leaders might evaluate emerging areas for 2027 planning cycles—without treating hype cycles as budgets.

## Purpose of a rolling roadmap

A roadmap is not a prediction. It is a prioritized set of experiments and policy updates aligned to business risks and opportunities. Revisit quarterly; archive items that stall for lack of sponsor or measurable outcome.

## Candidate themes under review

### Post-quantum and cryptographic agility

Organizations with long-lived data and embedded systems should inventory cryptography dependencies and plan for algorithm migration timelines published by standards bodies. Early work is discovery: where certificates live, which vendors support hybrid modes, and which contracts require vendor cooperation.

### Edge AI and distributed inference

Not every inference belongs in a central cloud region. Manufacturing, retail, and field operations may need low-latency models with offline tolerance. Open questions for this draft include reference architectures for model updates, telemetry privacy, and failure modes when edge nodes desync.

### Sustainable infrastructure

Energy use and hardware lifecycle increasingly appear in procurement and disclosure requirements. Metrics under consideration: utilization efficiency, carbon-aware scheduling where providers expose it, and extending asset life through refurbishment programs.

### Interoperability and data portability

SaaS consolidation creates lock-in risk. Roadmap items may include exit playbooks, open API requirements in new contracts, and internal abstraction layers where multi-vendor strategies are justified.

## Evaluation criteria (draft)

Each theme should score against:

- Business impact within twenty-four months
- Regulatory or security pressure
- Cost and skill availability
- Dependency on external ecosystem maturity

Themes scoring low on impact but high on risk—such as cryptographic migration—may still require foundational work without flashy projects.
